We see tea as a composition.

HOC is a tea house built on blends. We don’t sell a single leaf. We sell ingredients that complete one another:

a base, a flower or fruit to carry it, and one small touch that sets the balance.

Small batches, weighed one by one.

Every recipe in the collection was developed by us. The blends are made by hand in small batches, and each ingredient is weighed on its own, so every batch pours the same cup as the one before.

Everything in the cup has a job.

What matters in a blend is not how many ingredients go in, but what each one adds to the others. Every recipe is built on three principles.

  • Visible ingredients

    Rose and jasmine go in as buds, fruit in pieces. Look into a blend and you can see what is in it.

  • Balance

    In every blend one ingredient leads and the others support it. No spice and no sharpness is allowed to cover the rest of the cup.

  • Consistency

    Recipes are written as ratios and weighed by the gram. The cup you serve today is the cup you will serve three months from now.

Before you brew

Measure
3–4 g of blend for a 200 ml cup. For a pot, multiply by the number of cups.
Heat
Blends with green or white tea never meet boiling water. At 75–85 °C the cup opens without turning bitter.
Time
Every blend’s own temperature and steeping time is on its page.
