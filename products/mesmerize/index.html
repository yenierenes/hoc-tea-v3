Every ingredient has a job.

Mango and pineapple do the sweetness, lemongrass keeps it from turning syrupy, and butterfly pea pulls the whole glass toward deep blue. Best over a lot of ice.

  • Mango Sweet. Ripe stone fruit.
  • Pineapple Sharp. Bright, resinous.
  • Lemongrass Citrus. Lemon rind, grass.
  • Butterfly Pea Flower Gives the cup its blue, and little flavour of its own.

Brew it your way.

A cup is 200 ml and 3–4 g of leaf. Choose how you are serving it and how many cups; the numbers follow.
